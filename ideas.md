# leemethod.com — Design Brainstorm

## Context
A tribute and educational website for T. Joseph Lin, PhD — Head of R&D at Max Factor and pioneer of Low Energy Emulsification (LEE). The audience is a mix of the general public, cosmetic chemists, formulators, and students. Tone: clean, scientific, personal, and legacy-worthy.

## Idea 1 — "Laboratory Notebook" (Scientific Archival)

**Design Movement:** Mid-century scientific documentation meets modern editorial design.

**Core Principles:**
- Authenticity through archival texture — the site should feel like a beautifully preserved scientific record.
- Structured information hierarchy inspired by academic journals and lab notebooks.
- Restraint and precision — every element earns its place.
- Warmth through sepia and cream tones to honor the historical legacy.

**Color Philosophy:** Warm ivory (#F8F4EC) backgrounds with deep charcoal (#1A1A1A) text. Accent in a muted teal-green (#3D7A6F) evoking laboratory glassware and chemical solutions. A secondary amber (#C8882A) for highlights, referencing aged paper and gold award plaques.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial layout. Left-heavy text columns with a persistent right-side "margin" for annotations, pull-quotes, and small diagrams — mimicking a lab notebook. Full-width section breaks use subtle ruled lines.

**Signature Elements:**
- Faint graph-paper grid texture on section backgrounds.
- Thin serif rule lines as section dividers.
- Circular "seal" badges for awards and publications.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Deliberate and unhurried. Hover states reveal additional context (like annotations appearing in the margin). Scroll-triggered fade-ins that feel like turning pages.

**Animation:** Slow, graceful fade-and-rise on scroll entry. No bouncing or elastic effects — only smooth, linear transitions that feel scholarly.

**Typography System:**
- Display: *Playfair Display* (serif, authoritative, classic)
- Body: *Source Serif 4* (highly readable, academic)
- Labels/Captions: *IBM Plex Mono* (technical, precise)

## Idea 2 — "Precision Science" (Clean Modernist) ← SELECTED

**Design Movement:** Swiss International Typographic Style meets contemporary scientific communication.

**Core Principles:**
- Clarity above all — information is the hero, design serves it.
- Strong typographic hierarchy using scale and weight contrast, not decoration.
- Generous whitespace as a structural element.
- Subtle depth through shadow and layering, not color complexity.

**Color Philosophy:** Near-white (#F7F8FA) background with near-black (#111827) text for maximum readability. A single, precise accent color: deep cobalt blue (#1B4F8A) — the color of scientific precision, trust, and depth. Secondary: a cool slate (#64748B) for supporting text. Thin gold (#B8962E) lines used sparingly for award/legacy callouts.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric grid with a strong left-anchored vertical rule. Navigation is a slim, persistent top bar. Content sections alternate between full-width and constrained columns, creating visual rhythm without monotony. The hero is split: large typographic statement on the left, a striking portrait/image on the right.

**Signature Elements:**
- A thin vertical cobalt rule running down the left side of key sections.
- Molecular/emulsion diagram illustrations as decorative section dividers.
- Numbered section labels (01, 02, 03) in small caps.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Precise and responsive. Hover states are subtle color shifts and underlines. No gratuitous animation — motion is used only to guide attention.

**Animation:** Staggered slide-up on scroll entry. Navigation items have a clean underline-draw on hover. Section numbers count up on scroll entry.

**Typography System:**
- Display: *DM Serif Display* (elegant, authoritative serif for headings)
- Body: *DM Sans* (clean, modern, highly legible)
- Mono/Labels: *JetBrains Mono* (for chemical formulas, citations, and technical labels)

## Idea 3 — "Emulsion in Motion" (Scientific Visualization)

**Design Movement:** Data visualization aesthetics meets science communication design.

**Core Principles:**
- Make the invisible visible — emulsion science is beautiful and should look that way.
- Dynamic diagrams and animated illustrations as primary visual language.
- Layered information: accessible on the surface, deep on exploration.
- Bold contrast between dark backgrounds and luminous content areas.

**Color Philosophy:** Deep navy (#0D1B2A) background with crisp white (#FFFFFF) text. Accent in electric cyan (#00C9C8) evoking microscopy imagery and fluid dynamics. Secondary: soft lavender (#A78BFA) for legacy/tribute sections, adding warmth to the scientific palette.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed dark sections with floating "card" content panels. Horizontal scroll for the timeline section. Animated SVG diagrams of emulsion droplets as section backgrounds.

**Signature Elements:**
- Animated emulsion droplet SVG in the hero section.
- Microscopy-style circular frames for photos.
- Glowing data-point markers on the timeline.

**Interaction Philosophy:** Exploratory and immersive. Scroll triggers reveal animated diagrams. Hovering over technical terms shows tooltip definitions.

**Animation:** Particle-like droplet animations in the hero. Smooth morphing transitions between sections. Timeline markers pulse gently.

**Typography System:**
- Display: *Space Grotesk* (geometric, modern, scientific)
- Body: *Inter* (neutral, highly readable)
- Mono: *Fira Code* (for formulas and technical content)

## Selected Design: **Idea 2 — "Precision Science"**

Clean, authoritative, and deeply respectful of the scientific legacy. The Swiss modernist approach ensures the content is always the hero, while the cobalt blue accent and DM Serif Display typography give the site a distinguished, timeless quality appropriate for honoring a pioneering scientist.
